How it works

1. Click Start Recognition — the system watches your webcam.

2. Perform a sign — the green border lights up when motion is detected.

3. Pause briefly — the system treats the pause as a sign boundary.

4. The recognized sign appears in the sentence builder above.

5. Keep signing — each new sign is appended to build a full sentence.

6. Click Speak to hear the assembled sentence.
